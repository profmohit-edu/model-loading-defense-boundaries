# Search and selection
Twenty-four sources form the numbered corpus: six published papers, one public security assessment and seventeen official technical documents. The corpus is purposive and contrasts mechanisms; it is not an exhaustive systematic review.

ArXiv full texts were used as accessible versions of accepted/published papers where primary publication identity was verified. Five formal DOI identities were checked against Crossref registration records; the USENIX paper was checked against its official proceedings. The unverified-publication safetensors adoption and DITTO reports were read for horizon checking but excluded from the numbered references. They are not primary support for the synthesis.

The initial Picklescan raw URL under the Hugging Face organization failed; the official project repository was then located as mmaitre314/picklescan and retrieved. Python 3.12, PyTorch 2.6 and NumPy 2.3 documentation are deliberately version-specific. Remaining living documents are content-hashed snapshots, not immutable version claims. The older safetensors audit is read with its fix-review section, which records nine resolved issues and one unresolved informational issue as of that review.

AI assistance was used in search, extraction and appraisal. There was no independent human screening or adjudication. No corpus-wide frequency, detector ranking or vulnerability-currentness claim is made.
